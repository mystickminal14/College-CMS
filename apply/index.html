    DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

    After completing the online application process, applicants are required to submit
    the following documents to the Admission Office

    BACHELOR

    • Attested photocopy of SLC marks sheet and character certificate
    • Attested photocopy of 10+2 marks sheet or equivalent transcript
    • Character certificate
    • Migration certificate

    MASTERS

    • Attested photocopy of SLC marks sheet and character certificate
    • Attested photocopy of 10+2 marks sheet
    • Bachelor level marks sheet
    • Provisional pass & degree certificate
